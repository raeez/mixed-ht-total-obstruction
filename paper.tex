\begin{abstract}
A local binary operation does not by itself determine a global all-arity
open--closed theory.  We formulate the missing step as a complete filtered
Maurer--Cartan problem and identify the obstruction encountered when a
solution is lifted through one filtration stage.  Application to a concrete
mixed holomorphic--topological theory requires the construction of the
relevant descent algebra and is left open.
\end{abstract}

\section{The governing lifting problem}

Let $(\mathfrak g,F^\bullet)$ be a complete descending filtered differential
graded Lie algebra over a field of characteristic zero, with
\[
 \mathfrak g\cong\varprojlim_r\mathfrak g/F^r\mathfrak g,
 \qquad [F^a\mathfrak g,F^b\mathfrak g]\subset F^{a+b}\mathfrak g,
 \qquad dF^a\mathfrak g\subset F^a\mathfrak g.
\]
For $\alpha\in F^1\mathfrak g^1$, write
$\mathcal F(\alpha)=d\alpha+\tfrac12[\alpha,\alpha]$.

\begin{theorem}[One-stage obstruction]
Suppose $\mathcal F(\alpha)\in F^{r}\mathfrak g^2$ for $r\geq2$.  Its class
\[
 o_r(\alpha)=[\mathcal F(\alpha)]\in
 H^2(F^r\mathfrak g/F^{r+1}\mathfrak g,d_\alpha)
\]
is defined, where $d_\alpha=d+[\alpha,-]$.  A correction
$\beta\in F^r\mathfrak g^1$ with
$\mathcal F(\alpha+\beta)\in F^{r+1}\mathfrak g^2$ exists if and only if
$o_r(\alpha)=0$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On $F^r\mathfrak g/F^{r+1}\mathfrak g$, the term $[\alpha,-]$ vanishes,
so $d_\alpha$ induces the differential $d$.  Equivalently,
$d_\alpha^2=[\mathcal F(\alpha),-]$ vanishes on this quotient because
$[F^r\mathfrak g,F^r\mathfrak g]\subset F^{2r}\mathfrak g\subset
F^{r+1}\mathfrak g$.
The Bianchi identity gives
$d_\alpha\mathcal F(\alpha)=0$.  Modulo $F^{r+1}$, changing $\alpha$ by
$\beta\in F^r\mathfrak g^1$ changes its curvature by $d_\alpha\beta$;
the quadratic term $[\beta,\beta]/2$ lies in $F^{2r}\subset F^{r+1}$.
Thus the curvature can be killed at the next stage precisely when its class
is exact.
\end{proof}

\section{The mixed theory still to be constructed}

For a mixed holomorphic--topological application, the desired
$\mathfrak g$ must totalize local operations, open--closed actions, centre
maps, cover descent, counterterms, and comparison homotopies in one declared
grading and completion.  Naming these components separately does not prove
that their brackets close or that the inverse limit is derived-complete.

\begin{problem}
Construct this filtered algebra for one nontrivial Stein or formal local model,
compute its first nonzero obstruction class, and either construct compatible
primitives at every arity or prove that no such global lift exists.
\end{problem}
