# Maurer–Cartan Obstruction Towers in Completed Totalizations

Raeez Lorgat

A positive additive filtration turns the Maurer–Cartan equation into successive central lifting problems. This paper constructs the completed Thom–Whitney carrier for a semicosimplicial diagram of complete filtered differential graded Lie algebras and distinguishes the limits of compatible representatives, gauge classes, and primitives. A Hochschild calculation exhibits the obstruction to deforming a comparison map; examples show why completeness, separation, and filtration hypotheses matter. Application to a mixed holomorphic–topological theory requires its specific diagram and higher comparison operations.
