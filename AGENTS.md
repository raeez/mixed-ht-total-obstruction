# Agent Rules

## Golden rule: no project management at any scale whatsoever in the manuscript

Reader-facing sources contain mathematics or physics only, from title and
metadata through every proof, caption, footnote, and appendix. Never insert
agents, prompts, critiques, audits, workflows, tasks, commits, tests, progress,
ownership, or revision history. Mathematical status must remain explicit.

Archive material is an untrusted source of questions. Verify every carrier,
degree, completion, sign, map, and theorem independently. Seek correct,
rigorous, complete mathematics. Use the problem-first structural virtues of
Witten's major 1980s papers without imitating their language. Pass these rules
to every subagent.
